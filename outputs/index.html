CSV field guide

Header row required; names are case-insensitive. Example rows below explain format only and are not loaded as data.

Aircraft
aircraft_id,aircraft_type,unit,status,last_seen
Maintenance
work_order_id,aircraft_id,task,status,due_date,priority,opened_at,return_to_service
Telemetry
aircraft_id,timestamp,sensor,value,unit,threshold

Telemetry needs at least one of threshold or 5 readings per sensor for alerts.

Parts
part_number,part_name,quantity_on_hand,reorder_level,location

Supported status labels include available, serviceable, maintenance, grounded, open, in progress, closed, or complete.
